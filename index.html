Privacy Policy

📅 Last updated: May 16, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Nearmate ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information about you when you use our mobile application Nearmate ("the App").

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information — username, email address, phone number, date of birth, gender
  • Profile Information — profile photo, bio, Instagram handle, interests
  • Location — your approximate location to show nearby events and users
  • Verification Selfie — a selfie photo taken during profile verification (stored securely)
  • Messages — chat messages sent between users within the App

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information — device type, operating system version
  • Usage Data — features you use, events you join or host
  • Firebase Analytics — anonymous usage statistics to help us improve the App

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Show you nearby events and users based on your location and interests
  • Enable communication between users through in-app chat
  • Verify your identity through selfie verification
  • Send you notifications about events, friend requests, and messages
  • Improve and develop the App
  • Ensure the safety and security of our users

4. Location Data

Nearmate uses your device's location to show you events and users near you. Location is only collected when you are using the App. We do not track your location in the background. Your approximate location (city/area level) may be visible to other users on the App.

5. Profile Verification

If you choose to verify your profile, we collect a selfie photo. This photo is:

  • Processed on your device using facial recognition technology
  • Compared to your profile photo to confirm your identity
  • Stored securely in our cloud storage (Firebase Storage)
  • Never shared with third parties
  • Used solely for the purpose of verifying your identity on Nearmate

6. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share your information with:

  • Firebase (Google) — our backend infrastructure provider
  • Other Users — your username, profile photo, interests, and location are visible to other Nearmate users
  • Legal Requirements — if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users

7. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase, located in the asia-south1 (Mumbai, India) region. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

8. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal reasons.

9. Your Rights

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — update your personal information through Edit Profile
  • Deletion — delete your account and personal data through Settings
  • Withdraw Consent — withdraw location access through your device settings

10. Children's Privacy

Nearmate is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete that information.

11. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase — firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google ML Kit — developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
